takeaways
+ almost，几乎、差一点就.......(通常表示某件事差一点发生，但实际上没有发生)
  ```text
  almost died         # 差一点就死了
  almost every day    # 几乎每天
  almost blind        # 几乎看不见/几乎失明
  almost ate meat     # 差一点就吃肉了
  ```
+ nearly，几乎、将近、差不多........(通常表示已经发生，但还没完全达到)
  ```text   
  nearly nothing      # 几乎没有任何东西
  nearly 100 peoples  # 差不多 100 人
  nearly midnight     # 几乎深夜了
  nearly impossible   # 几乎不可能
  nearly finished     # 差不多完成了
  ```
+ could't + 动词原型
  ```text
  I could't open my eyes.
  I could't eat meat.
  ```
+ keep + 宾语 + 形容词，让某物保持........状态
  ```text
  keep your room clean.
  keep her eyes open.
  keep the door open.
  ```
+ because + who + do something(谁干了什么事)
  ```text
  because it's raining.
  because i'm eating.
  ```
+ because of + something
  ```text
  because of the rain.
  because of eating.
  ```
